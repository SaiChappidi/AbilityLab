# Champion portrait artwork

Ahri, Morgana, Ezreal and Mel portraits and Q/W/E/R ability icons © Riot Games. Downloaded from Riot's official Data Dragon static asset CDN, version 16.22.1, for this local unofficial demonstration. Portraits are static arena tokens and HUD portraits, with a circular display mask. They are not animated character models or sprite sheets. Arena, minion, turret and effect artwork remains original procedural placeholder art; sound cues are generated in code.

- [Morgana portrait](https://ddragon.leagueoflegends.com/cdn/16.22.1/img/champion/Morgana.png)
- [Ezreal portrait](https://ddragon.leagueoflegends.com/cdn/16.22.1/img/champion/Ezreal.png)
- [Mel portrait](https://ddragon.leagueoflegends.com/cdn/16.22.1/img/champion/Mel.png)
- [Official Data Dragon documentation](https://developer.riotgames.com/docs/lol#data-dragon)

Files live in `Assets/Resources/Dex/Portraits` and `Assets/Resources/Dex/Abilities`. Exact URLs for all sixteen ability icons are recorded in the latter directory's `Sources.txt`. The asset version does not set simulator ability or item balance. Mel's current-kit interpretation is informed by the [official 26.22 patch notes](https://www.leagueoflegends.com/en-gb/news/game-updates/league-of-legends-patch-26-22-notes/), with explicitly simplified demo tuning.
