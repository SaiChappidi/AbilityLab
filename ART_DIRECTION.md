# Dex visual direction

Dark navy instrument panels, cyan interaction feedback and warm gold highlights carry forward the existing Dex palette.
The arena is a clean tactical test chamber: circular champion tokens, restrained geometry and clearly colored ability zones.
The workbench presents a developer's request, source change and evidence with generous spacing, readable typography and a visible run timeline.

The customer is the engineer investigating ability regressions. The arena supports that workflow. The product does not depend on live-League visual fidelity or a larger champion roster.
